# ⚡ Tata Power EV Charging Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Tata Power EV charging operations to understand charging demand, station utilization, customer behavior, and financial performance. The objective is to turn charging data into meaningful business insights that can support operational improvements and future infrastructure expansion.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze EV charging usage and business performance.
- Understand station utilization and operational efficiency.
- Explore customer behavior and charging demand patterns.
- Evaluate financial performance and expansion opportunities.
- Present key insights through interactive dashboard visualizations.

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

- **Business Performance:** Charging activity, revenue, and usage trends.
- **Station Efficiency:** Utilization patterns and station-level performance.
- **Customer Analysis:** Customer segments and charging preferences.
- **Financial Analysis:** ROI, payback period, and expansion planning.

- Data Visualization

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis helps present charging network performance in a structured and visual format. It supports the identification of demand patterns, station efficiency opportunities, customer behavior trends, and potential areas for business growth.
