📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure
performance, customer usage, station utilization, and financial
profitability for Tata Power.
The objective is to build a comprehensive four-page Excel dashboard that
helps management evaluate charging demand, operational efficiency,
customer behavior, financial performance, and expansion opportunities
across districts. The analysis combines operational, customer, demand,
and financial metrics to support data-driven infrastructure investment
and expansion decisions.
🎯 Business Problem
Tata Power needs to evaluate the performance and expansion potential of
its EV charging infrastructure. This analysis addresses questions such
as:
- How is EV charging demand evolving over time?
- Are charging stations handling demand efficiently?
- Which stations are overutilized or underutilized?
- How does customer behavior vary across segments?
- Which districts and projects deliver the strongest financial
  returns?
- Where should new charging infrastructure be deployed?
- What is the gap between projected EV demand and available charging
  capacity?
📊 Project Scope
Target users: Senior management, strategy teams, and operations
managers.
The project covers four business areas:
1. Business & Usage Overview
2. Station Utilization & Operational Efficiency
3. Customer & Demand Behavior
4. Financial Performance & Expansion Strategy

1. Business & Usage Overview

This page summarizes charging demand, energy consumption, revenue
generation, and customer activity.
Key KPIs - Total charging sessions - Total energy consumed (kWh) -
Total revenue - Active customers - Average revenue per session - Average
kWh per session
2. Station Utilization & Operational Efficiency

This page compares station performance and infrastructure utilization
across districts and stations.
Key KPIs - Sessions per station - Average utilization per station -
Plug count and actual plug usage - Average kWh per station -
District-wise station density - Sessions-per-plug ratio
3. Customer & Demand Behavior

This page explores customer segments, charging behavior, income tiers,
EV models, and battery capacities.
Key KPIs - Unique customers - Sessions per customer - Average kWh
per customer - Revenue per customer - Usage by income tier, car model,
and battery capacity
4. Financial Performance & Expansion Strategy

This page evaluates project profitability, investment returns, payback
periods, and future charging-infrastructure opportunities.
Key KPIs - Project CAPEX - Annual revenue and EBITDA - Payback
period with and without subsidy - Five-year ROI - Existing stations and
projected EVs - Projected plugs and demand-supply gap - Expansion
priority tier
