## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Tata Power's EV charging infrastructure to understand charging demand, station utilization, customer usage patterns, revenue generation, and financial profitability.

The project consists of four Excel dashboard pages that provide insights into business performance, operational efficiency, customer behavior, and future infrastructure expansion opportunities.

The objective is to transform raw data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The project addresses the following business questions:

- How is EV charging demand changing over time?
- Which charging stations are highly utilized or underutilized?
- How efficiently are charging plugs being used?
- What are the charging patterns of different customer segments?
- Which districts offer better financial returns?
- Where should new EV charging stations be established?
- Is the existing charging infrastructure sufficient for projected EV demand?

## 📊 Project Scope

The dashboards are designed to support:

- Senior Management
- Business Strategy Teams
- Operations Managers
- Financial Planning Teams

The analysis covers four major areas:

1. Business and usage performance
2. Station utilization and operational efficiency
3. Customer behavior and charging demand
4. Financial performance and expansion strategy
